About Meridian

Twenty-five years of building one continuous line of care.

Meridian Health started as a single clinic with one idea: a patient's record, history, and care team should never be scattered across separate buildings. That idea is still what we build toward today.

2001
The year our first clinic opened its doors
Our story

From one waiting room to a connected network.

A community clinic with three doctors and one shared filing cabinet grew, department by department, into a network of four campuses. What never changed is the rule we started with: your file follows you, not the other way around.

  • Founded in 2001 as a single community clinic
  • Grew to 4 campuses and 12 accredited centers
  • Now serving 50,000+ patients a year
What drives us

Mission and vision.

Our mission

To make coordinated, specialist-level care available on a timeline that matches real life — not the other way around. Every process we build starts with one question: does this get the patient to treatment faster?

Our vision

A future where "referral" doesn't mean "start over." Where a specialist across the network already knows your history before you say a word. We're building the infrastructure for that today.

History

Two and a half decades, in five moments.

2001

The first clinic

Three doctors, one building, and a promise to keep every patient's record in one place.

2008

Second campus, first specialists

Cardiology and orthopedics joined as dedicated departments, connected to the original clinic's records.

2015

Digital records, network-wide

Every campus moved onto one shared patient record system — the technical backbone of "care that moves with you."

2020

Telehealth, permanently

What began as a stopgap became a standing option — consult by video when a visit isn't necessary.

2026

Four campuses, one network

150+ specialists, 12 accredited centers, and an average emergency response time under 8 minutes.
